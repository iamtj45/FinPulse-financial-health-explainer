The current ratio and operating cash ratio can tell different stories in
the same year, and the gap is informative.

A company with a strong current ratio but a weak operating cash ratio may
be liquid on paper — sitting on receivables and inventory — while
struggling to convert operations into actual cash. That combination is
worth more attention than either ratio alone, since it's often an early
sign of collection problems or a slowing business before it shows up
elsewhere.
