Operating margin and net margin usually move together, but when they
diverge sharply it's worth asking why.

A company with a healthy operating margin but a much lower net margin is
typically carrying heavy interest expense from debt, a large one-time
charge, or an unusual tax situation — none of which reflect how the core
business is actually performing.

Conversely, a net margin propped up above operating margin (a one-time
gain, a tax credit) is not a repeatable result and shouldn't be read as an
improving trend.
