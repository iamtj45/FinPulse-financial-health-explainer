Profitability ratios measure how much of each revenue dollar a company
keeps at different stages — after production costs, after operating costs,
after everything.

Read together rather than individually, the four profitability ratios in
this tool (gross, operating, and net margin, plus return on equity) show
where in the cost structure profit is being made or lost. A company with
strong gross margin but weak operating margin has a cost problem in
overhead or R&D, not in what it sells; a company with strong operating
margin but weak net margin has a financing or tax story, not an operating
one.
