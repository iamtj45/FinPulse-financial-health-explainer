Comparing gross margin across sectors is one of the most common
misreadings of this ratio.

Retail and grocery businesses typically run 20-35% because they resell
physical goods bought from suppliers at a markup. Manufacturers land in a
similar range once raw materials and labor are counted. Software, media, and
other businesses that sell a product with near-zero marginal cost to
reproduce routinely post 70-90%.

A 30% gross margin is unremarkable for a retailer and alarming for a
software company — the number only means something next to same-sector
peers.
