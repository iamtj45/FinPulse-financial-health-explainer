A single year of negative free cash flow is easy to misread as distress.

Capital-intensive businesses — telecoms building out networks, utilities
building generation capacity, retailers opening stores, any company
mid-expansion — go through years where capex spikes well above operating
cash flow by design.

The three-year trend matters more than any single year here: a company with
consistently negative free cash flow across multiple years, with no large
one-time capex program to point to, is a different and more concerning case
than one dip during an announced expansion.
