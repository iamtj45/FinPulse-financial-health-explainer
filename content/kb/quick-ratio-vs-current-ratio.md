A wide gap between the current ratio and the quick ratio is itself
informative: it means a large share of current assets is tied up in
inventory.

That's expected and benign for a retailer around its inventory build-up
before a holiday season, but a growing gap over successive years can mean
inventory is piling up faster than it's selling — a working-capital problem
rather than a strength, even though the current ratio alone would look fine.

Read the two ratios together, not in isolation.
