A profitability trend matters more than a profitability level, because it
captures direction: is the business getting more or less efficient at
turning revenue into profit.

A declining margin trend alongside flat or growing revenue often points to
rising input costs, pricing pressure from competitors, or a shift toward
lower-margin products or services. An improving trend alongside flat
revenue can indicate cost discipline or a favorable pricing shift. Margins
that stay flat for years in a competitive industry aren't a bad sign —
stability itself is a data point.
