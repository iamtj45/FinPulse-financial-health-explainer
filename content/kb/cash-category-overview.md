The cash ratios in this tool are built from the cash flow statement rather
than the income statement or balance sheet, which makes them harder to
distort with accounting choices — cash either moved or it didn't.

Free cash flow, FCF margin, and operating cash ratio together describe
whether a business generates enough actual cash to fund itself, cover its
near-term obligations, and have something left over. A company can be
profitable on paper (positive net income) while being cash-negative, and
this category is where that gap becomes visible.
