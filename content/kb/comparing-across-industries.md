The single most common way to misread these ratios is comparing a company
against a different-sector benchmark instead of its own peers.

A 25% gross margin is ordinary for a grocery chain and worrying for a
software company; a 2.0 debt-to-equity ratio is routine for a utility and
aggressive for a retailer. Every ratio in this tool is more informative
compared against the same company's own history, or against companies in
the same sector, than against a generic "good ratio" number that doesn't
account for what kind of business is being measured.
