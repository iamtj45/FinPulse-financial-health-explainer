Equity ratio and debt to equity describe the same balance sheet from two
angles and will usually agree, but they diverge in one important case:
negative equity.

Debt to equity becomes undefined and not meaningful when equity turns
negative, but equity ratio keeps working — a negative equity ratio is
exactly the signal that debt to equity can't show.

When you see a "not meaningful" debt-to-equity value, check the equity
ratio for the same year; it's often the more informative number in that
situation.
