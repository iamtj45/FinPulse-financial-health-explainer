Utilities and other regulated, capital-intensive infrastructure businesses
finance long-lived physical assets (power plants, pipelines, grids) with
substantial long-term debt, supported by predictable, often regulated
revenue.

Debt-to-equity ratios of 1.5-2.5 that would be a warning sign at an
unregulated company are normal and expected here, because the cash flows
backing that debt are unusually stable. Growth in this sector is also
capital-expenditure heavy, so free cash flow is frequently low or negative
during active construction periods without indicating distress.
