Real estate investment trusts (REITs) carry structurally high leverage
because their business model is owning income-producing property financed
substantially with mortgage debt, and they're required to distribute most
of their taxable income as dividends rather than retaining it to pay down
debt or build equity organically.

Standard debt-to-equity and equity-ratio thresholds calibrated for
operating companies read as alarming applied to a REIT; sector-relative
comparison matters more here than almost anywhere else in this tool's
coverage.
