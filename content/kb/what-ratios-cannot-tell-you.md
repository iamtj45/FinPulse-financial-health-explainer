---
id: what-ratios-cannot-tell-you
tags: [general, scope]
---

These ratios describe a company's financial position as reported in its
statements — they cannot tell you about the quality of its management, the
durability of its competitive position, pending litigation, regulatory
risk, or how its industry might change.

Two companies can post identical ratios across the board and face very
different futures for reasons no financial ratio captures. This tool is a
starting point for understanding financial health as reported, not a
complete picture of business quality or risk.
