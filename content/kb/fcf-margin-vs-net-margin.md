Net margin and FCF margin measure different things and a gap between them
isn't a red flag by itself.

Depreciation and amortization reduce net income without using cash, so a
capital-intensive business can show FCF margin above net margin.
Conversely, a company investing heavily in growth capex can show healthy
net margin while FCF margin runs low or negative, because that spending
doesn't hit the income statement as an expense the way it hits the cash
flow statement as an outflow.

Look at the two together rather than expecting them to match.
