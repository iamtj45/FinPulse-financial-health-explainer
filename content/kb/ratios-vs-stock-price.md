---
id: ratios-vs-stock-price
tags: [general, scope]
---

Every ratio in this tool is computed from a company's financial statements —
what it owns, owes, earns, and spends — and none of them incorporate the
company's stock price, valuation multiples, or market sentiment.

A financially healthy company by these measures can be expensively or
cheaply priced by the market; these ratios say nothing about whether a
stock is a good purchase at its current price, because that question
depends on the price paid, not just the business's underlying financial
condition. This tool explains financial health, not investment value.
