A rising debt-to-equity trend isn't automatically bad — it often
accompanies a deliberate decision to fund growth, an acquisition, or a
share buyback with debt rather than diluting shareholders.

What matters is whether the increase is matched by the cash flow and
earnings to service it. A rising leverage trend paired with declining or
flat operating margin and free cash flow is a materially different
situation than the same rising leverage paired with strong and growing
cash generation.
