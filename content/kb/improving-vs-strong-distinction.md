id: improving-vs-strong-distinction
tags: [general, trend]
---

A ratio's trend and its level are two different signals and shouldn't be
collapsed into one story.

A ratio can be improving while still weak — a company climbing out of a
difficult period — or declining while still strong — a company coming off
an unusually good year toward a more normal one. Both cases are common and
neither the trend nor the level alone tells the full story; the two
together are more informative than either in isolation.
