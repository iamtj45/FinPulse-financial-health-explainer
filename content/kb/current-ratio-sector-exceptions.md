Grocery and restaurant chains collect cash from customers immediately but pay
suppliers on 30-60 day terms, so they can run current ratios below 1.0
indefinitely without distress. Walmart and Costco have both operated for
years with ratios near 0.8-0.9.

Software and SaaS companies tend to run the opposite way: minimal inventory
and large deferred-revenue cash balances produce current ratios of 3-5 that
don't indicate idle capital the way they would at an industrial company.

When this ratio reads unusually low or high, check the sector before
treating it as a signal either way.
