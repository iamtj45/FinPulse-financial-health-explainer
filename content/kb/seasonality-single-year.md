id: seasonality-single-year
tags: [misinterpretation, general]
---

A single fiscal year is a snapshot, and businesses with seasonal demand,
cyclical end markets, or lumpy large-contract revenue can look meaningfully
different depending on exactly when their fiscal year ends relative to
their business cycle. A retailer's fiscal year-end just after a strong
holiday quarter will show a different balance-sheet picture than one ending
mid-year.

This is part of why this tool shows a three-year trend rather than a single
year's ratios in isolation — but three years is still a short window for
businesses with longer cycles, and a single unusually strong or weak year
can still skew what "improving" or "declining" means.
