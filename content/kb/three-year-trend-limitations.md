---
id: three-year-trend-limitations
tags: [general, trend, scope]
---

This tool's trend classification compares the earliest and latest of the
last three to four fiscal years of available data. That's enough to
separate a genuine multi-year direction from a single unusual year, but
it's still a short window for industries with longer cycles — commodities,
heavy industry, real estate development — where a full cycle can run five
to ten years.

A "stable" or "improving" trend over three years doesn't rule out a
longer-term cyclical pattern that a longer lookback would reveal.
