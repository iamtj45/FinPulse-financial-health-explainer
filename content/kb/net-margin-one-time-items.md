A single quarter or fiscal year of unusually high or low net margin is often
the result of a one-time event — an asset sale, a legal settlement, a
restructuring charge, a tax law change — rather than a change in the
underlying business.

Because this tool computes ratios from as-reported net income without
adjusting for one-off items, a sharp single-year spike or drop in net margin
is a prompt to look at what happened that year, not necessarily evidence the
business got better or worse.
