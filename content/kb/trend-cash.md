Because free cash flow can swing year to year with the timing of a single
large capital project, a one-year decline is common and often unremarkable
(see the capex-cycle note).

A cash trend declining across three consecutive years, especially alongside
stable or growing revenue, is a more reliable signal that something
structural has changed — rising costs, slowing collections, or a business
that increasingly needs more capital to generate the same revenue.
